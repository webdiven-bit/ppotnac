Brief History

Founded in the year 2025, P.POTNAC MEDFARM LTD set out to give quality care, competence in behaviour, accountability, and convenience of medical and agro services — because distance is never a barrier.

We professionally turn farm products into delicious food as value-added processing, quality control, and effective marketing on your behalf. Our professionalism is saving by avoiding harm and people centred by responding to individual needs and values.
